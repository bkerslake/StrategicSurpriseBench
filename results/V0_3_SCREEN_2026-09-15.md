## Findings from this screen

Luna’s lower bound exceeds GPT-4o’s upper bound under the existing accepted grades. This separates those models within this run even under the most favorable assignment of missing grades to GPT-4o. It is not a population-level or expert-validated ranking.

Both Meridian Shock variants received 6/10 for GPT-4o and 10/10 for Luna, with complete grades. In Black Current variant b, GPT-4o inferred a possible coordinated Karsian operation and a deliberate challenge despite incomplete ownership evidence. The judge’s intended adaptation grade was withheld because its quote changed curly apostrophes to straight ones. No quote normalization or re-grading was applied after seeing results.

Sonnet’s public-health sessions were filtered by its provider, and other sessions had malformed or ungrounded judge output. Its missingness is too large for a useful relative ranking. No target output reached the generation token cap.

## Run settings and limits

- Six fictional crises, two variants each, two prose assessments per session.
- One epoch: 72 target calls and 68 judge calls were recorded. Four judge calls were skipped for Sonnet’s two filtered Red Harvest sessions.
- Target output cap: 4096 tokens; prompts request no more than 600 words.
- GPT-4o snapshot: `gpt-4o-2024-11-20`, temperature 0.2. Luna reasoning effort and Sonnet effort: medium.
- Judge: `anthropic/claude-opus-5`, 4096-token cap, provider-default effort. Initial grading never sees later evidence or the second response.
- Positive grades require exact response quotes. Missing or ungrounded judgments remain missing; no model mean is reported unless all 12 sessions are fully graded.
- Common Anthropic judging may introduce provider-family bias; human checks are needed before interpreting close differences.
- No human labels, repeated runs, or statistical claims of model superiority.
- Source hashes, settings, raw responses, and full judge decisions are retained in the private run directory. These scores are incompatible with v0.2.
