## Findings from this screen

This is the first screen under the v0.3.1 anchors, judge instructions, quote normalization, and
bounded judge retries. It is a fresh run: every target response and judge decision is new. Scores
are not comparable with the v0.3.0 screen of the same day.

Three of four models are fully graded, with 60 of 60 accepted grades each. Sonnet's ten missing
grades all come from provider filtering of both Red Harvest sessions, which is a target-side
issue the grading changes do not address. No grade was lost to a malformed judge response or an
ungrounded quote: three first-attempt judge calls returned invalid grade objects and were
recovered on retry, and one accepted grade needed punctuation normalization to match its quote.
Seven judge retries in total recovered nine grades across the four models.

Under the tightened anchors the ceiling has moved down and the spread has opened. GPT-4o earns
mostly ones. Sol still earns full credit on 50 of 60 grades, so the anchors have not resolved
the top of the range; Luna and Sonnet sit between. Uncertainty and adaptation remain the
dimensions most models reach 2.00 on, and adversarial thinking is the lowest for every model.

These remain provisional model grades from one run over six scenario families with a common
Anthropic judge. They have not been validated by expert reviewers.

## Run settings and limits

- Six fictional crises, two variants each, two prose assessments per session.
- Recorded target calls: 96; judge calls: 99.
- Target output cap: 4096 tokens; prompts request no more than 600 words.
- GPT-4o snapshot: `gpt-4o-2024-11-20`, temperature 0.2. Luna/Sol reasoning effort and Sonnet effort: medium.
- Judge: `anthropic/claude-opus-5`, 4096-token cap, provider-default effort. Initial grading never sees later evidence or the second response.
- Positive grades require response quotes matched after punctuation normalization. Missing or ungrounded judgments remain missing; the unqualified model mean is reported only when all 12 sessions are fully graded.
- Common Anthropic judging may introduce provider-family bias; human checks are needed before interpreting close differences.
- No human labels, repeated runs, or statistical claims of model superiority.
- Source hashes, settings, raw responses, and full judge decisions are retained in the private run directory. These scores are incompatible with v0.2.
